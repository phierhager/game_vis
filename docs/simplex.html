Population games · three strategies

Triangle flows

One population, three strategies. The population's mix is a point in a triangle whose corners are the pure strategies. Each strategy grows when it earns more than the population average: the first-order signal of a population. Click inside the triangle to follow a population from any starting mix.

Reading the triangle

Every point is a population mix; the distance to an edge is the share of the strategy in the opposite corner. Tracers follow the replicator flow (or the rule you pick). Markers are rest points: filled if nearby mixes are pulled in, open if they are pushed away, half-filled for saddles, a ringed dot where orbits circle, a diamond where the linearisation cannot decide.

Three kinds of rock-paper-scissors

With zero-sum payoffs the replicator dynamics keeps the product xRxPxS constant, so every orbit is closed. Let a win pay more than a loss costs and that product grows: populations spiral into the mixed equilibrium. Make losses hurt more and it shrinks: the population spirals out and spends longer and longer near each pure state in turn, a heteroclinic cycle.
